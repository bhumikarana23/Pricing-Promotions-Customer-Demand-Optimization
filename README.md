# Pricing, Promotions & Customer Demand Optimization

## Project Overview
An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project focused on understanding pricing strategies, promotional effectiveness, customer purchasing behaviour, and product demand in a fashion retail environment.

The project uses a synthetic fashion e-commerce dataset to explore business performance, identify customer segments, evaluate promotional patterns, and generate data-driven recommendations for pricing and demand optimization.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze product pricing, discounts, revenue, and profitability.
- Evaluate promotional campaign performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour and segmentation.
- Examine customer retention and cohort trends.
- Analyze product demand and inventory movement.
- Develop actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data exploration, cleaning, analysis, and reporting.
- **SQL:** Data querying, joins, aggregations, and business KPI analysis.
- **Python:** Data cleaning, exploratory analysis, customer segmentation, and demand forecasting.
- **Power BI:** Interactive business intelligence dashboards.

## Dataset
The project consists of seven interconnected datasets:

| Dataset | Records |
|---|---:|
| Customers | 5,000 |
| Products | 1,000 |
| Orders | 12,000 |
| Order Items | 18,246 |
| Promotions | 40 |
| Sessions | 40,000 |
| Inventory | 18,000 |

*All records are synthetic and created for analytical and educational purposes.*

## Key Areas of Analysis
1. Pricing and discount analysis
2. Revenue and profitability analysis
3. Promotional performance evaluation
4. Customer segmentation using RFM analysis
5. Customer retention and cohort analysis
6. Product demand forecasting
7. Inventory and business performance analysis

## Project Structure
- `Dataset/` – Original datasets.
- `Cleaned_Data/` – Processed datasets.
- `Excel/` – Excel analysis and reports.
- `SQL/` – SQL scripts and queries.
- `Python/` – Python analysis and outputs.
- `PowerBI/` – Interactive dashboards.

## Business Value
The project demonstrates how structured data analysis can support better pricing decisions, promotional planning, customer retention strategies, and demand management.

**Note:** This is an independent portfolio project using synthetic data. It is not affiliated with or based on proprietary Myntra data.
