# Power BI Dashboard – Pricing, Promotions & Customer Demand Optimization

## Overview
This folder contains the Power BI dashboard developed for analyzing pricing strategies, promotional performance, customer behaviour, and product demand in a fashion e-commerce business.

The dashboard is designed to convert business data into interactive visual insights that support commercial and operational decision-making.

## Dashboard Analysis Areas

### 1. Business Performance
- Revenue performance
- Order trends
- Sales performance
- Profitability indicators

### 2. Pricing & Promotions
- Discount distribution
- Promotional performance
- Revenue across discount categories
- Promotion type comparisons

### 3. Customer Analytics
- Customer purchasing behaviour
- Customer segmentation
- Customer retention patterns

### 4. Product & Demand Analytics
- Category-level performance
- Product demand trends
- Inventory movement
- Demand-related insights

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Interactive Visualizations

## Objective
Provide an interactive business intelligence solution that helps stakeholders understand business performance, evaluate promotional strategies, and identify opportunities for pricing and demand optimization.
